4Receipt Scanning & Spending Protection (Optional)

4.1 Receipt Scanning & OCR (Optional)

When you choose to scan or import a receipt (JPG, PNG, or PDF up to 2 MB; up to 2 receipts/month on the Free plan and 30 receipts/month on Pro), Align requests camera or photo/file access only when you initiate a scan or upload.

  • Local file storage: Your receipt image or PDF file (e.g. receipt_*.jpg) is saved locally on your device and is not uploaded to Supabase Storage.
  • On-device OCR: Text extraction runs locally on your device to pre-fill merchant name, amount, currency, and dates.
  • Synced metadata: Only structured receipt metadata (merchant name, amount, currency, document/payment/due dates, file name, file type, file size, local path reference, and optional notes) is synced to your Supabase receipts table.

4.2 Spending Protection (Optional, Android)

Spending Protection is an optional Android feature that helps warn you before overspending when you open a supported payment app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or Amazon Pay).

  • Permission: Requires your explicit grant of Android Usage Access (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) in system settings.
  • Local processing only: Align queries UsageStatsManager strictly on-device over a short recent window (about 20 seconds) solely to check whether one of the supported payment apps was opened in the foreground, compares your local monthly expense total against your 90% budget threshold, and displays a local notification.
  • No upload or tracking: App usage events are never stored, logged, uploaded to Supabase, or shared with any third party. You can disable Spending Protection at any time in Settings or revoke Usage Access in Android system settings.

5SMS Transaction Tracking (Optional, Android)

Optional feature. Align is a personal income and expense management app, not the default SMS app, and does not execute financial transactions. All core Align features remain usable without SMS permission. On Android devices without telephony/SMS hardware, SMS Tracking is disabled.

Permission & explicit consent

Align requests only the android.permission.RECEIVE_SMS permission when you choose to enable SMS Tracking in Settings → SMS Tracking, and only after displaying an in-app prominent disclosure and receiving your explicit consent. Align does not request READ_SMS, SEND_SMS, RECEIVE_MMS, or RECEIVE_WAP_PUSH.

Real-time processing only (no inbox scanning)

When enabled, Align listens solely to new incoming SMS broadcasts in real time. Align never scans, reads, or indexes historical SMS messages stored in your device's SMS inbox.

Filtering & raw SMS handling

  • Raw SMS content: Parsed strictly in device memory and never stored on disk, logged, or uploaded to our servers.
  • Filtered messages: OTPs, verification codes, promotional messages, failed or declined transaction alerts, and non-financial messages are filtered out and discarded immediately.

Local processing vs. synced transaction records

For detected financial transactions, Align extracts only structured fields: transaction type (expense, income, or bill reminder), amount, currency, merchant/title, category, date, and optional card/account last-4 digits or reference ID.

  • Local encrypted queue: Pending extracted items are staged strictly on your device in noBackupFilesDir (excluded from Android Auto Backup) using AES-256-GCM encryption (AES/GCM/NoPadding, 128-bit authentication tag) with a key stored in AndroidKeyStore (hardware-backed TEE/StrongBox where supported by the device), with no plaintext fallback.
  • 14-day deduplication hash: To prevent duplicate entries, a deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint derived from structured transaction fields (optional account last-4 digits, amount, transaction type, and reference ID or title and date — never the raw SMS body) is stored locally in the encrypted deduplication file for 14 days and is never uploaded.
  • Pending Reviews: Uncertain, ambiguous, or logged-out transactions are placed in Pending Review in Settings for your confirmation before recording.
  • Supabase sync: Only structured transaction records (title, amount, currency, category, date, and optional reference note) that are auto-recorded while signed in or confirmed by you are saved locally and synced to your Supabase account.

How to disable SMS Tracking

You can withdraw consent and disable SMS Tracking at any time from Settings → SMS Tracking in the app, or revoke the SMS permission in your Android device's system app settings.

6Financial Data You Store

Financial data in Align is entered manually by you, captured via optional Receipt Scanning (see §4), or recorded via optional Android SMS Transaction Tracking (if enabled, see §5). We store your recorded entries in our Supabase PostgreSQL database to sync across sessions and provide analytics, reminders, and reports.

Data categories stored

  • Expense Reminders — title, amount, currency, category (Subscriptions, Business, Card & EMI, Insurance, Utility, Other), due date, recurrence frequency, reminder settings, notes, payment history
  • Income Entries — title, amount, currency, source (Salary, Freelance, Business, Rental, Other), date, recurrence, notes, payment history
  • Manual Expenses — amount, currency, category, date, notes
  • Payment Logs — occurrence dates and amounts paid, linked to parent reminder or income entry

Multi-currency display

Align displays amounts in the currency you select for each entry. We do not perform live currency conversion. Your profile stores a default display currency preference used across the app.

Offline access

Align caches your data locally on your device using MMKV encrypted storage so the app works when you are offline. Pending changes are queued and synced to the server when connectivity is restored.

  • Quarterly — 90 days of Pro access
  • Yearly — 365 days of Pro access

If you purchase another plan while Pro is still active, the new duration stacks onto your remaining expiry date. Prices are fixed per currency and are not calculated using live exchange rates. You can view pricing for your selected currency on the Sales screen.

8Push Notifications

Align uses Expo Notifications to deliver bill reminders and income alerts. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device based on:

  • Your configured reminder-before preferences (same day, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days)
  • Your selected reminder time (e.g. 9:00 AM)
  • Your account timezone (set in Settings → Timezone)

Permission

We request notification permission during onboarding. You can grant or deny permission, and you can change this at any time in your device Settings. If you deny permission, reminders are delivered only to the in-app Notification Inbox.

Push token

Your Expo push token is stored in our database linked to your account and the specific reminder it belongs to. It is used only to deliver your own scheduled reminders. We do not use push tokens for marketing messages.

Disabling notifications

You can disable all notifications in Settings → Notifications. Toggling this off cancels all scheduled local alarms. Notifications can be re-enabled at any time.

9Analytics & Export Reports

All analytics displayed in the Analytics tab and all CSV export reports are generated entirely from your own financial data in Align (whether entered manually or recorded through optional features you enabled). We do not share this data with analytics services or advertising platforms.

CSV Export (Pro only)

Pro subscribers can export their financial data as a CSV file covering up to 12 months. Exported files are generated on demand, delivered directly to your device, and are not retained on our servers after delivery.

No third-party analytics SDKs

Align does not embed third-party analytics SDKs such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar tools. We do not track your in-app behaviour for marketing or advertising purposes.

10Data Retention & Deletion

Active account

We retain your data for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period thereafter to allow you to reactivate.

Notification logs

Notification logs are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Individual item deletions

When you delete an expense, receipt metadata record, or scheduled reminder, it is removed immediately from your local app cache and deleted from Supabase (and local receipt files are unlinked from device storage). When you permanently delete an income entry, it is soft-deleted (hidden immediately) and purged after 30 days by the nightly cleanup job.

After Pro expiry or account deactivation

  • When your Pro plan expires or you deactivate your account via Settings → Data & Privacy → Deactivate Account, your account enters a 180-day reactivation window (data_available_until).
  • After 365 days from account deactivation or Pro plan expiry, reminders and notification logs for the account are permanently purged from the database by an automated nightly cleanup job.

Direct account & data deletion requests

You can review deactivation and deletion steps at any time at dev-dhavalp.github.io/align-docs/account-deletion.html.

If you request immediate permanent deletion by emailing alignapps4@gmail.com, we process full personal data erasure within 30 days. Payment transaction metadata may be retained for up to 7 years as required by applicable financial recordkeeping laws.

Our automated cleanup schedule runs nightly:
• 1:00 AM — plan expiry sweep
• 1:30 AM — inactive reminder archiving
• 2:00 AM — permanent purge of soft-deleted and expired records

11Data Security

  • Encryption in transit — All communication between the app and our servers uses TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS).
  • Encryption at rest — Your data is stored in Supabase's PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest enabled on the underlying infrastructure.
  • Local storage — Data cached on your device is stored using MMKV encrypted storage (Android Keystore). Pending Android SMS transactions and 14-day deduplication hashes are stored locally in noBackupFilesDir using AES-256-GCM encryption backed by AndroidKeyStore.
  • Row-level security — Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure each user can only read and write their own data. No user can access another user's records.
  • Auth tokens — Supabase manages session tokens using secure JWT-based authentication. Refresh tokens are rotated automatically.
  • Webhook verification — Razorpay payment webhooks are verified using cryptographic signature checks to prevent payload forgery.
  • No third-party data brokers — We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to any third party for commercial purposes.
